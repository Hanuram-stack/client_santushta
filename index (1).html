How We Learn

Every Student Doesn’t Need the Same Explanation.
They Need the Right One.

Generic tutoring rushes through chapters to check off syllabus boxes. Our sessions focus on deep conceptual understanding through two proven, student-first frameworks:

Framework 01 Diagnostic First

The Student Gap Check

Diagnose Before Delivering

Before diving into any chapter, we identify where the student actually stands using a rapid diagnostic triage:

1

What you already know well

Solid foundation to build upon; reinforced with brief confidence checks rather than redundant drills.

2

What you partially understand

Where misconceptions quietly hide and lead to unexpected exam errors.

3

What you are missing completely

Where the conceptual bridge needs to be established step-by-step from base principles.

“No wasted time re-explaining mastered concepts, and no jumping ahead before fundamentals click.”

Framework 02 Active Retention

The Explain It Back Method

True Understanding Leaves No Room for Guesswork

Passive listening creates an illusion of understanding. In Santushta’s sessions, once a concept is taught, the student explains it back in their own words:

Step 01

Learn

Clear, structured explanation with relatable real-world science examples.

Step 02

Explain

Student articulates the concept back in their natural vocabulary without memorized jargon.

Step 03

Apply

Solve non-standard numericals and board-pattern application questions independently.

Step 04

Remember

Long-term retention built through self-formulated concept schematics and summaries.

“If you can’t explain it simply, you don’t understand it yet. Once you can, the exam is painless.”

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Clear, transparent answers about sessions, pedagogy, scheduling, and guidance.

1. What subjects does Santushta teach?
Santushta teaches Physics, Chemistry, and Biology at the school level, alongside focused board examination preparation, academic guidance, and general career guidance for students navigating STEM subject streams.
2. Which curriculum boards are covered?
Sessions are tailored to your specific curriculum and board—just let Santushta know your board and class when you register, and the first session will be structured around your actual syllabus.
3. How are online tutoring sessions conducted?
Online sessions run over Google Meet with interactive digital whiteboarding, so concepts are worked through visually and in real time, just like an in-person session.
4. Is offline (in-person) tutoring available?
Yes, offline sessions are available subject to location and schedule availability. Mention your preference when you register and Santushta will confirm feasibility.
5. What is the ₹25 Consultation session?
It’s a focused 1:1 conversation—over Google Meet or a phone call—to help you step back, assess where things stand, and leave with clear, actionable priorities on exam prep, subject roadblocks, or academic direction.
6. Is the ₹25 session therapy or mental health counselling?
No. This session provides educational guidance, study planning, and peer-level academic mentoring. It is not a clinical medical, psychological, or mental health counselling service.
7. How does “The Student Gap Check” work?
Before any chapter begins, Santushta runs a rapid diagnostic to identify what you already know well, what you partially understand, and what you are missing completely—so no time is wasted re-teaching mastered concepts or rushing past shaky fundamentals.
8. What is the “Explain It Back” method?
After every concept is taught, the student explains it back in their own words, then applies it to non-standard problems and builds their own summary for long-term retention—a four-step Learn, Explain, Apply, Remember cycle.
9. What are the regular class hours?
Monday to Friday, 5:00 PM – 9:00 PM IST, with weekends available flexibly by appointment.
10. Can parents attend or receive feedback?
Yes, parents are welcome to stay informed of progress. Just mention your preference when registering and Santushta will share regular updates alongside the student.
11. How do we reschedule a session if an emergency arises?
Simply reach out over WhatsApp or email as early as possible. Santushta will work with you to find the next available slot within the regular session timings.
12. What happens after I submit the registration form?
Santushta reviews each request personally to ensure a good pedagogical fit, and you’ll hear back within 24 hours via WhatsApp or Email to confirm your first session.
